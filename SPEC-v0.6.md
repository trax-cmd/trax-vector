### 2.2 The camera (gl.js is the only converter)
`view = { x, y, zoom, rot, rect: {x, y, w, h}, shake: {x, y} }`. With `cx = rect.x + rect.w/2`, `cy = rect.y + rect.h/2` and `k = zoom`:
- rot 0: `sx = cx + (wx − x)·k + shake.x`, `sy = cy + (wy − y)·k + shake.y`.
- rot ±1 (portrait): `sx = cx − rot·(wy − y)·k + shake.x`, `sy = cy + rot·(wx − x)·k + shake.y` (west rot −1: lane 0 on the LEFT, his gates at the BOTTOM, +x is UP).
`R.toWorld(view, px, py)` and `R.toScreen(view, wx, wy)` are the inverse pair; drag.js, minimap.js, the labels, edge pan and the probe call them and nothing else converts. `uCam` is built from the same formula (physical px = css × dpr, dpr capped at 3).

**FOLLOW.** `state.follow = { lane, free, x, y, orderedAt, hot }`. The followed point of lane L: for 6 s after an order in L its drop x; else the centroid of L's deaths in the last 3 s if `lanes[L].kills3s > 0`; else `(frontW + frontE)/2`; clamped to [1300, 7700] unless the lane's enemy gate is dead, then the yard keep's x. y = LANE_Y[L]. Dead zone: the camera moves only when the followed point leaves the middle 50 % of the rect, then lerps 0.08 a frame (≈ 300 ms). Lane to follow: the lane last ordered; after 12 s with no order the hottest lane (max kills3s); at the bell the centre lane. A one-finger field drag sets `free = true`; a minimap tap, a strip lane-arrow tap, a plate tap or a double-tap on the field re-follows in 200 ms (a lerp of 0.25). A `wave` plate for lane L or a `shatter` in L moves the follow to L **unless a drag is live or an order was given within 6 s** (the camera never moves under a live drag except edge pan).

### 2.4 The body shader (gl.js SHAPE_VS/FS rewritten)
Instance stream, 16 floats: `aA (x, y, r, shape)` · `aFill (r, g, b, a)` · `aEdge (r, g, b, rot)` · `aX (band, mark, flash, state)`. FS: `aa = fwidth(d)·0.75`; `fill = (1 − smoothstep(−aa, aa, d + ow))·fillA` in the fill colour; `line = smoothstep(−aa, aa, d + ow)·(1 − smoothstep(−aa, aa, d))` at alpha 1.0 in the edge colour; `seam = (1 − smoothstep(−aa, aa, d − ow·0.67))·smoothstep(−aa, aa, d)` at `#0A0D18` alpha 0.8 (two touching bodies of one side keep a dark 1 px gap). **No exp glow term.** Ring family: `sdRing` band = `aX.band` (0.18 body ring, 0.05 VFX ring) with the outline rule on both edges. Shapes: 0 circle, 1 box (no rotation: `rot = age·0.9` deleted in paint), 2 triangle (heading-rotated), 3 hexagon (no rotation), 4 ring, 5 diamond stretched 1:1.6 along the heading (`p.y *= 0.62` before `sdDiamond`).
**Inner marks (`aX.mark`, a second distance, edge colour, 1.5 css px lines inset 1.5 px, alive at the floor size):** 1 PUPIL filled disc r 0.30 (2 px at 5 px) · 2 PLATE square outline at 0.5 r · 3 SPINE apex→base-centre line · 4 CORE filled hexagon r 0.35 (3.5 px at 10) · 5 ORBIT one 2 px dot at angle `uTime·6.283` on the ring · 6 SLIT centre line along the long axis · 7 CRACKS three 2 px dark lines across the core (a stronghold under 50 %) · 8 DASHED outline (8 px dash, 50 % duty, rotating 12 Hz; a cloaked body to its own side) · 0 none.
**State bits (`aX.state`, integer):** SHIELD 1 (a second outline 2 px outside, 1 px, `#BFE3FF` at 0.35 + 0.5·sh/shieldMax — the paint pass passes the ratio in fillA's spare range: paint calls `out.body` twice, the second with shape 4 band 0.05 NOFLOOR at r+2 px; the bit is informational) · STUN 2 (outline flickers white at 12 Hz) · SURGE 4 (a 2 px gold ring at r + 2 px pulsing 1.0→0.5 at 2 Hz plus a 1 px white rim outside the outline) · BEATS 8 (edge → `#5CFF7A`, outline 2 px, plus a 2 px ring at 1.8 r, min 7 px) · LOSES 16 (the same in `#FF4D4D`) · DIM3 32 (fill alpha × 0.3) · DIM4 64 (fill × 0.4) · CLOAK 128 (fill 0.25, mark 8) · NOFLOOR 256 · CAP2 512. x = CP_X[slot] on the lane centre-line. **Presence:** hp of alive bodies within 260 whose `U.lane` equals the point's lane (`wa` west, `wb` east). **ONE CAPTURE RULE:** a side alone on a point moves `prog` toward its own sign at **0.5/s while prog carries the enemy's sign, 0.25/s otherwise** (an enemy point: 2 s to 0 + 4 s to ±1 = 6 s; a neutral point 4 s); both present = frozen (a `contested` event once per contest start); the owner changes only at ±1 (`capture` event with `from`). **ORDER RULE:** the west may gain on slot s only if s = 0 or `owner[cp(l, s−1)] === 0`; the east only if s = 4 or `owner[cp(l, s+1)] === 1`; a side lacking the prerequisite still freezes the point by presence. **Pay:** 6/s a held point, 12/s THE CENTRE (a full board 108/s against 30/s from strongholds). **RALLY:** every 5th tick allies of the owner within 300 heal 2 % of max hp per second. **Surge fill:** a capture +800, the centre +1,200 to the capturer.

### 3.4 The front (in the sim, read everywhere)
Per lane, `frontW` = the midpoint between the last west-held slot (prefix from slot 0) and the next point (the gate 1100 → CP0 when none: 1500; CP4 → the east gate when all five: 7500); `frontE` mirrored (none 7500, all 1500). `S.front = { w: [3], e: [3], chevW: [3], chevE: [3], moved: [3] ({ team, at }) }`, recomputed in `stepCheckpoints`; a change emits `front { lane, team, dir: +1 | −1, x }` (an advance for the capturer, a fall-back for the loser, both emitted on a strip).

### 3.5 Bodies in lanes and the void
`U.lane` (Uint8) is fixed at muster. In `move()`, after the velocity step: `if (inRun(nx)) ny = clampY(lane, ny, r)`, plus an inward push of 60 wu/s when within 2 r of the band edge (the soft edge); in the yards no clamp. **Targeting is lane-locked:** `pickNearest` skips `j` when `U.lane[j] !== U.lane[i]` and either body is in the run; shots and blasts hit whatever they touch. A body in the void is impossible; a drop in the void snaps to the nearest band (drag.js, and the sim clamps too).

### 3.6 The deploy command and NO PARKING
`{ op: 'deploy', team, batt: id | deckIndex, lane, x, free? }` and, for the wire and tools, `{ op: 'deploy', team, tower, batt | kind, goal }` mapped: a goal `≥ 1000` (a checkpoint index + 1000) → its lane and x; a gate → its lane and x; a keep → `towerLane(keep)` and x beyond the enemy gate. `kind` deploys spawn one body at the lane's own gate with the same march. **Muster point:** the lane's own gate; if dead, the nearest living keep of that side (the body walks the yard to `(GATE_X[team], LANE_Y[lane])` first); if none lives on that side, refused. The formation forms `MUSTER_AHEAD = 220` wu ahead of the gate (toward the enemy), sorted heavy-first as now, **clamped to the band at muster**: any slot with `|side| > HALF − big − 20` is folded to that limit and pushed back one rank (`forward −= sp`); a cloud's radius is capped at `HALF − big − 20`. Price `round(cost·mult)`, `mult = 1 + time/240`. The drop x is clamped to [1300, 7700]; `x` past the enemy gate's x means "to the keeps" and is allowed only when that lane's enemy gate is dead (else clamped to the gate). Per body: `U.goalX` (Float32), `U.holdUntil` (Float32), `U.quiet` (Float32), `U.stage` (Uint8), `U.defend` (Uint8). A drop within 100 of a checkpoint the side holds is a DEFEND (`defend = 1`).
**The march (replaces the 'walk to the point and hold' rule in move(); the nearest enemy in aggro always overrides):**
- stage 0 MARCH to `(goalX, LANE_Y[lane])` (via the lane head if mustered at a keep); arriving within `60 + 12·(grp % 5)` → stage 1, `holdUntil = time + (defend ? 45 : 20)`, `quiet = 0`.
- stage 1 HOLD at the point: `quiet += dt` when no target, else 0. Leaves when (a) the point is a checkpoint and `owner === team` (off for DEFEND), or (b) `time ≥ holdUntil`, or (c) `quiet ≥ 8` (off for DEFEND) → stage 2.
- stage 2 ADVANCE: `goalX` = the next checkpoint toward the enemy with `owner !== team` beyond the body's x; on arrival → stage 1 with a 20 s hold (rule (a) on). None left → stage 3.
- stage 3 THE GATE: `U.goal = gate(1−team, lane)`, target `−2 − goal` as today. Gate dead → stage 4.
- stage 4 THE KEEP: `U.goal` = the nearest living enemy keep by straight distance from the dead gate; walk through the yard (no clamp). None → stage 5 IDLE (stand). `retarget()` moves a body from 3 to 4 the tick its gate dies.

### 3.7 The opening and the clock

The war ends when all five strongholds of a side fall (after the doom wave, §5.3) or at 480 s on total stronghold hp, tie by checkpoints held, tie by kills. `stepCheckpoints` runs every 5th tick with `dt·5` as `stepWells` does today.

`advise(sim, team, snap) → { verb, lane, x, role, answers, card, why } | null` with `verb ∈ DEFEND | ALARM | SURGE | BREAK | TAKE | PUSH`. ## 8. THE CAPTAIN (bot.js on `snap.lanes[]`)
Every look (NORMAL 2.5 s = 75 ticks, HARD 45, EASY 120) per lane: `PRESSURE = enemy fielded − own fielded`, `DEPTH = |GATE_X[own] − enemy front|`. Rules in order: (1) DEFEND THE WEAK LANE: any lane with DEPTH < 1,500 or `gateHitAgo < 3` → the counter to that lane's top enemy role dropped at its own front x, up to 60 % of the purse, at most 2 cards; (2) MASS: on every WAVE tick (`TEMPERS[t].wave` s: easy 55, normal 40, hard 30) pick the lane with the highest own advantage (−PRESSURE), tie the enemy's shallowest front, and announce a wave of 2–4 cards (burst by temper: easy 2, normal 3, hard 4) — the first an ARMOR when the enemy's top role there is SWARM or STRIKE, the rest counters — mustered 3 s later at its own front x; (3) COUNTER-PUSH: if the enemy dropped 3+ cards or surged in lane L within 6 s and own DEPTH in L > 3,400, the wave goes to the lane where the enemy has the LEAST fielded energy, not L; (4) SURGE: `surge ≥ 10000`, a lane holds ≥ 40 % of its fielded energy and that lane's front is within 1,300 of an enemy checkpoint or gate → `{ op: 'surge' }`; (5) THE CENTRE: no threat and no wave due → one card to the lane whose centre is neutral or enemy and least defended, at x 4500; (6) the opening: one card per lane at tick 1 (burst 3 on the first look). Reserve 10 %. `TEMPERS = { easy: { incomeM, every: 120, burst: 2, wave: 55 }, normal: { every: 75, burst: 3, wave: 40 }, hard: { every: 45, burst: 4, wave: 30 } }` with `incomeM` re-set by `tools/tempers.js` (targets NORMAL 4-4 of 8, EASY 7-8, HARD 1-2 for the coached player). The wire's `{ tower, goal }` deploy is mapped (§3.6) and `tools/relay.js` also accepts `{ op: 'surge' }`.
